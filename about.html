The studio

Two people.
One big idea.

Cactus Patch Games is an independent game studio founded by Jeff and Kate, a husband-and-wife team working out of the Texas Hill Country. We started CPG because we wanted to make the kinds of games we grew up loving — games with character, heart, and enough challenge to keep you coming back.

Our first title is CottonTail, a third-person action platformer about a scrappy rabbit and her mech companion taking on whatever the world throws at them. It's the game we've been building toward, and we can't wait to get it into your hands.

We're a small studio by choice. Every decision — design, code, art, business — gets real attention because there's no one to hand it off to. We think that shows in the work.
